title = "Oiling a wooden handle"
date = 2026-11-12
tags = ["tools", "finishes"]
+++

Linseed oil keeps ash and hickory handles from drying out and cracking. Wipe
on a thin coat, wait twenty minutes, then wipe off everything that has not
soaked in.

Rags soaked in linseed oil can catch fire as they dry. Lay them flat outside
or keep them in a sealed metal tin.
