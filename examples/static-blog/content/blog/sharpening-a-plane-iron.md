A dull plane tears wood instead of cutting it. Sharpening takes ten minutes
once the back of the iron is flat.

<!-- more -->

## What you need

| Item | Grit | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coarse stone | 1000 | Set the bevel |
| Fine stone | 6000 | Polish the edge |
| Strop | — | Remove the wire edge |

## Steps

1. Flatten the back on the coarse stone until it shines along the edge.
2. Hone the bevel at 25° in a guide, then a 30° micro-bevel.
3. Polish on the fine stone and strop.

> A sharp iron shaves end grain without crushing it.
