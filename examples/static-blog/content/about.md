This blog is written by A. Writer, who repairs old tools and writes down what
works.

## How this site is made

- Pages are Markdown files with a short TOML header.
- `engine build` turns them into HTML in `public/`.
- The look comes from TagBrush.css and the behaviour from TagAction, both
  bundled in the engine and served from this site.
